Frequently Asked Questions

Is a printhead overhaul worth it compared to buying new?

In most cases yes. An overhauled printhead costs significantly less than a new part and achieves the same performance.

How long does a printhead repair take?

Turnaround time is usually a few working days. In urgent cases we offer 24h service at no extra charge.

Which printer manufacturers are supported?

We support all common matrix printer manufacturers: Epson, Fujitsu, IBM, Siemens, PSI, OKI, Olivetti, Tally and many more.

Can I send in my printhead?

Yes, you can send your printhead by post. Within a 5 km radius we also offer free pick-up and delivery.

Thermal transfer printheads – can these be overhauled?

Unfortunately, overhauling thermal transfer printheads is technically not possible. However, we can offer you affordable new heads. Available models include: Tally, PSI, Dascom, Siemens, PP803, PP806, PP405, PP408.

Printhead defective?

Call us or visit our online shop – we help quickly and straightforwardly.
